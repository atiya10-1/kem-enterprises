# KEM Enterprises Catalogue Mapping

This build follows the supplied Jhula, Signage and Ducting catalogue visual hierarchy.
Prominent catalogue section headings are categories. A/B/C coded catalogue entries are products. Size, finish, colour, voltage, wattage, packing and warranty information are stored as catalogue specifications/variants rather than invented categories.
